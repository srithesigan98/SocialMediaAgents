# Creator Research — Raw Findings

**Research date:** 2026-07-16
**Method:** Direct fetch of threads.net/threads.com profiles returned HTTP 403 for every handle
(Threads blocks unauthenticated/non-JS scraping) — including on Hormozi's own profile root. All
findings below come from web search: search-engine-indexed Threads post snippets/URLs (some
containing verbatim post text), official bios, and cross-platform profiles (X/Twitter, YouTube,
TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn).

**Legend**
- **VERIFIED** — actual post text seen (indexed snippet/URL) or a directly-quoted bio/stat.
- **INFERRED** — based on descriptions from secondary sources or a closely related account, not
  directly observed post text.
- **UNKNOWN** — no reliable information found.

## 2. @stocktwits_top — handle unconfirmed; format INFERRED from verified sibling accounts

**Identity:** No account under the exact handle `@stocktwits_top` was found. The verified
accounts in this family are `@stocktwits` (official brand, 100.6K followers, 3.7K threads, bio:
"The global voice of finance, trading, and investing") and `@stocktwitsindia` (regional spinoff,
verified post text below). `@stocktwits_top` is most likely a similar regional/topical spinoff or
a fan-run aggregator mimicking the same format.

**Posting format (VERIFIED, from `@stocktwitsindia`/`@stocktwits` post text):** Short bulleted
"trending tickers" list, same template every time:

> Top Trending on Stocktwits right now 👇
> • Natco Pharma: -20%
> • Suven Pharma: +7%
> • Rainbow Children's Medicare: +2.5%
> ...
> See all thats trending on Stocktwits 👇 [link]

Single-ticker spotlight variant:

> "Tanla (+10%) is among the top trending stocks on Stocktwits right now, buzz is all about..."
> "Titan among the top trending stocks on Stocktwits right now — here's why it's trending"

**Hook patterns:** Data-dump hook (bulleted tickers + live % change as the hook itself,
screenshot-able); curiosity-gap hook ("X is trending... here's why 🔥"); 🔥 emoji flags the
day's hottest mover.

**Framework:** Pure aggregation/curation, no personal opinion — a **listicle + curiosity-gap**
template repeated verbatim with different tickers, multiple times a day.

**Topics:** Whatever's trending that day, sector-agnostic.

**Tone:** Neutral, data-driven, zero editorializing — reads like a wire service, not a
personality account.

**Video:** No evidence of native video; pure short-text + emoji + outbound link.

**CTA (VERIFIED):** "See all that's trending on Stocktwits 👇 [onelink.me URL]" — every post
drives traffic off-platform to the main app.

## 3. @cryptokaleo — VERIFIED identity + cross-platform presence; VERIFIED sample post text

**Identity:** "KALEO" — pseudonymous crypto trader/analyst active since Sept 2017, co-founder of
NFT project LedgArt. One of the best-known crypto-X callers from the 2021 bull run (early calls
on DOGE, SOL, AVAX). 718K+ followers on X, YouTube `@realcryptokaleo` (~7.6K subs), active
TikTok. Threads handle appears to be `@cryptokaleo.eth` (bio: "The only real Threads account for
CryptoKaleo" — impersonators exist), 3.5K followers, 146 threads — clearly a secondary channel
relative to X.

**Format:** Short punchy single-paragraph market calls, occasional chart references, no long
chains observed.

**Hook patterns (VERIFIED, real post text):**
- Terse hype/momentum: *"And there was the chaos in the charts we were looking for. No clean
  breakouts allowed. Run it back turbo. It's finally time to send out of this abysmal range."*
- Minimalist confidence: *"Feels like we're finally actually going for it"*
- Position-disclosure: *"Bought a bag of BTMX. It looks primed for continuation..."*
- Contrarian caution: opens reminding followers not to get trapped by bullish-looking altcoin
  charts — warns against the crowd's read right when sentiment is euphoric.

**Frameworks (INFERRED from multiple descriptions of his style as "charts + sentiment +
humor"):**
- **Call → chart reasoning → risk caveat** — states a position/prediction, briefly explains the
  technical/sentiment basis, sometimes appends a risk reminder.
- **Sentiment-check** — periodic pure "vibe check" posts on market mood, no specific ticker.
- **Position/"bags" disclosure** — states personal holdings, doubling as social proof and a soft
  trade idea.

**Topics:** BTC dominance, major alts (SOL, AVAX, DOGE, PEPE, LTC, BCH), macro cycle timing,
risk management/psychology.

**Tone:** Confident, terse, slang-heavy ("bags," "sending," "loading," "turbo"), first-person —
reads like a trader's live journal (his own X bio calls it that).

**Video:** YouTube does crypto technicals/macro discussion; TikTok presence confirmed but
specific format UNKNOWN.

**CTA:** None found in retrieved snippets — the calls themselves drive replies/reposts (people

## 4. @ringgitsidehustle — VERIFIED (multiple verbatim posts retrieved)

**Hook patterns (VERIFIED, exact text):**

  RM4k 25: RM4.1k 26: RM5.5k 27: RM8k 28: RM10k 29: RM11k 30: RM11k 31: RM12k 32: RM12.7k 33:

- Portfolio-breakdown reveal: *"My current investment assets: ASNB: RM35.1k, Stocks: RM131.7k,
  EPF: RM253.3k, Crypto: RM258.9k"*

3. **Universal-truth → personal-application** (everyone has 24 hrs → here's what I did → advice

**Topics:** Salary progression, net worth tracking, EPF/ASNB, stock vs. crypto allocation,
dividend income, side hustles, crypto risk warnings.

**Tone:** Casual, first-person, vulnerable/transparent, local humor, practical big-brother
advice-giving — reads as a peer sharing real numbers, not a guru selling a course.

**Video:** No evidence found — text/data-post-only.

**CTA:** None found explicitly — the vulnerability of sharing real numbers is the implicit

## 6. @hormozi (Alex Hormozi) — VERIFIED, strongest data of all — analyzed for technique only

Studied purely for hook/format/framework technique per the original brief — not a finance/
trading/crypto source, and none of his topics belong in Hulk's own content.

**Presence:** `@hormozi`, 1.1M followers, 3.6K threads.

**Format:** Overwhelmingly short, single-sentence or few-sentence aphoristic posts — self-
contained and quotable/screenshot-able without opening a thread. Occasional numbered-list posts.

**Verbatim examples (VERIFIED):**
- *"It's worth becoming someone who consistently disappoints the people who secretly hope you
  fail."*
- *"Your biggest threat isn't your competition, it's the mediocre version of you that you've
  settled for."*
- *"If you want to make more money, solve bigger problems."*
- *"If you're willing to work 10 years without a return, you can accomplish anything."*
- *"You'll look like a genius if you just sit down and ask yourself what happens next."*
- *"No matter how many decisions you've made, it's never too late to ignore everyone else."*
- *"The work works on you more than you work on it."*
- *"STEAL THIS 👇 This was my first post ever."* — meta post framing his own content as a
  template for others to copy.

**Hook patterns:**
1. Contrarian/reframe: "Your biggest threat isn't X, it's Y."
2. Conditional "If you..." structure — his single most repeated hook template.
3. Standalone aphorism — one complete thought, no setup, no elaboration.
4. Numbered-list framing for multi-point posts.
5. Permission/meta hook ("STEAL THIS") — invites imitation, doubles as authority play.

**Framework — his own stated "Hook → Retain → Reward" model** (corroborated by secondary
sources): Hook grabs attention (5 types he cites: past/life-lesson, recent-past/calendar-recap,
present/in-the-moment, trend-jacking, "manufactured"/crazy-idea); Retain uses the "greased
chute" — short sentences, no fluff, open loops; Reward delivers real actionable value at the end.
Most posts are the entire idea in one line — the "argument" happened elsewhere (video/podcast)
and the Threads post is the distilled takeaway.

**Production pipeline (VERIFIED via his own quote, *"We put out about 250 [pieces of content]
per week"*):** tests ideas as tweets first, tracks outsized-engagement ones, repurposes winners
into Threads/IG/YouTube Shorts/long-form — his Threads feed is a curated "best-of" of
pre-validated one-liners, not first drafts.

**CTA:** Mostly none — the line's quotability is the implicit CTA (people screenshot/reshare
unprompted). Exception: the meta-CTA "STEAL THIS 👇".
