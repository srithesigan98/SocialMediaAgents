# Framework: Price Anchor

Lead with the number that does the work, then justify it.

Line 1 — the price, alone, as a statement. ("RM480k. Cheras. 1,050 sqft.")
Line 2 — the comparison that makes it interesting (the `highlight` field).
Line 3–4 — two concrete specs (beds/baths, tenure, instalment).
Line 5 — the CTA line, verbatim.

Rules: no adjectives before the price. Never say "affordable" — show the instalment instead.
