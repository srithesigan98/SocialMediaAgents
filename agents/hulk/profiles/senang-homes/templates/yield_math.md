# Framework: Yield Math

For investor-facing listings where `rental_yield` or `monthly_instalment` exists.

Line 1 — the hook as a question of arithmetic ("Instalment RM2,150. Market rent RM2,600.")
Line 2 — what that gap means, stated plainly, no promises.
Line 3 — the property identity (title, location, size).
Line 4 — one risk or condition stated honestly (tenure, age, maintenance).
Line 5 — the CTA line, verbatim.

Rules: only use numbers present in the row. Never project appreciation.
