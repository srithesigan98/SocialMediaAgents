# Framework: Quick Spec Drop

A scannable listing post. Works well as the second post of the day.

Line 1 — one-line identity: type, deal, location.
Lines 2–5 — spec lines, one fact each, prefixed with a middle dot or dash.
Final line — the CTA line, verbatim.

Rules: max 5 spec lines. Drop any field the row doesn't have rather than writing "N/A".
