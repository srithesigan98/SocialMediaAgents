# MRTA vs MLTA: myths vs facts (~60 s, 9:16)

The four myths are provisional until the fact-check workflow returns; each storyline takes whichever four pass.

**Recommended: A, "The loan letter".** One RM500K loan carried through every myth is the worked example an
explainer needs, and it starts where the viewer already is: the offer letter their banker hands them.

## A. The loan letter (recommended)
- **Device / form:** a myth turned over plus one worked example (a RM500K loan); one frame that rebuilds.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a home-loan offer letter for RM500,000 fills the frame. Each myth arrives
  as a handwritten note pinned to it. The note gets a peach MYTH stamp and a strike-through, then the fact
  prints over it while the letter's own lines (cover amount, balance, who is paid) rebuild to prove it. The
  viewer feels they have just decoded the paper their banker will hand them.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is the letter with the handwritten note "MRTA pays my family" already
  pinned. At 0.6 s a peach MYTH stamp lands on it. Voice: "Your banker will offer MRTA. Here's what most
  buyers get wrong."
- **Beats:** hook (myth 1 stamped) → who gets paid (the payee line swaps from family to bank) → "my cover stays
  the same" (the RM500K figure drains into the reducing curve, while MLTA's line stays level) → "it moves when I
  refinance" (a second bank's letter slides in; the MRTA line tears off and the MLTA line carries across) →
  "it's compulsory" (the loan-condition clause is highlighted) → one-line claim → CTA "Comment MRTA".
- **Look:** an ivory paper letter on a forest-green ground. Outfit for the myths, Gelasio italic for the facts,
  terracotta strike-throughs and peach stamps. Everything drawn, no photos.
- **Sound:** a calm piano / lo-fi bed ducked under the voice, a soft stamp thud on each MYTH, a paper flip on
  each fact.

## B. Thirty years on one road
- **Device / form:** the life of one loan, where the road itself is the explanation; a flight through one world.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the camera travels a road from Year 0 to Year 30 past a small house. Each
  myth is a signpost met at the moment it matters: signing at Year 0, refinancing at Year 8, a claim at
  Year 10. The viewer feels the policy change over time, not as a definition.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is the Year 0 signpost reading "MRTA pays my family?", with the camera already
  moving. Voice: "Thirty years. One loan. Four myths."
- **Beats:** hook → Year 0, "compulsory?" → Years 1–30, the MRTA lane narrows while the MLTA lane stays wide →
  Year 8, the refinance fork → Year 10, the claim and who gets paid → "Pick by your plans" → CTA.
- **Look:** a top-down drawn road in forest and beige. A peach lane for MLTA, an ivory lane for MRTA, signposts
  set in Outfit.
- **Sound:** a travelling bed with a light pulse, a whoosh past each signpost, a chime on each fact.

## C. Myth or fact? (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** type carries the argument as a quiz; cut on the beat.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** giant statements fill the screen one at a time with a two-beat "MYTH or
  FACT?" countdown. The verdict then slams in on a colour-field change, with a one-line why and a tiny
  diagram. It plays like a game, and viewers post their score in the comments.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is "MRTA pays your family." in huge type on peach. At 1.0 s "MYTH or FACT?"
  pulses in. Voice: "Myth or fact: MRTA pays your family."
- **Beats:** statement → countdown → verdict and why, ×4, each in a new colour field and layout → "How many did
  you get?" → CTA "Comment your score + MRTA".
- **Look:** full-bleed colour fields (forest, peach, ivory, terracotta) that change every beat. Oversized Outfit,
  with Gelasio italic for the verdict lines.
- **Sound:** a punchier beat-driven bed, a tick on the countdown, a low buzz for MYTH and a chime for FACT.
