# The Watcher — Review Rubric

Grade the video on 8 dimensions, 1–5 each (max 40). Score from what you actually watched/heard, and cite timestamps. Pair every low score with a fix in the rebuild.

| # | Dimension | 1 (fails) | 3 (average) | 5 (elite) |
|---|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Hook (0–3s)** | Logo/intro/"hey guys"; no payoff | States the topic, mild curiosity | Contrarian/identity/open-loop payoff in <12 words, visual grabs frame 1 |
| 2 | **Retention design** | Slow, one flat shot, no reason to stay | Some pacing, an open loop | Constant motion, pattern interrupts, question that only the end answers |
| 3 | **Length & focus** | >45s or rambling, multiple ideas | 30–45s, mostly one idea | 21–34s, exactly one idea, no dead air |
| 4 | **Captions** | None, or tiny/static | Basic auto-captions | Bold word-by-word, one highlighted word, readable on mute |
| 5 | **Clarity of value** | Jargon, no takeaway | Teaches something, a bit buried | One crisp, memorable lesson a beginner keeps |
| 6 | **Credibility (visuals)** | Talking head only / fake charts | Some real chart, mixed | Real chart footage proving the point, clean framing |
| 7 | **CTA / engagement driver** | None | Generic "follow me" | Specific open loop or question that earns comments |
| 8 | **Discoverability** | No/irrelevant caption & tags | Decent caption, generic tags | Curiosity caption + niche + geo + broad tag mix tuned to audience |

## Verdict bands (total /40)
- **34–40 — Scale it.** This format works; make 5 more like it this week.
- **26–33 — Fix and repost.** Strong core, one or two leaks bleeding reach.
- **18–25 — Rebuild.** Right idea, wrong packaging; use the shot-by-shot rebuild.
- **≤17 — Scrap the format.** Keep only the lesson; rebuild from the hook up.

## Scoring discipline
- Anchor to `benchmarks.md` numbers, not taste.
- The hook (dim 1) and captions (dim 4) are the most common view-killers for trading pages — weight them heavily in the written verdict even though each is 5 points.
- If you couldn't retrieve audio (no Whisper key), score dims that need sound (1, 5, 7) from on-screen text + visuals and say the audio wasn't transcribed.
