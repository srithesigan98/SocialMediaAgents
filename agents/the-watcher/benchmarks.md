# Short-Form Video Benchmarks (2026)

The numbers The Watcher grades against. Sourced from creator-performance data across TikTok / Reels / Shorts, with a focus on finance/trading education. Update these as the platforms shift.

## Retention (the metric that decides reach)
- **3-second hold rate:** 60% minimum to get distributed; **70–80%** is viral territory.
- 50–60% of all drop-off happens in the **first 3 seconds**. Win or lose there.
- Of viewers who pass 3s, ~65% reach 10s; ~45% of those reach 30s.
- **Watch-through / retention for reach in 2026: 70%+** (the bar was 50% two years ago).
- TikTok weights *early hold* over *total length*: 80% held for 3s out-distributes 60% held for 30s.

## Length
- **Sweet spot: 21–34 seconds** for highest engagement.
- Reels in the 15–30s range average ~5.8% engagement.
- One idea per video. A 3-concept video is 3 videos.

## Hook
- Keep the spoken hook **under ~12 words**.
- Highest-retention hook types: **identity call, contrarian statement, open loop, confession.**
- Bad: "Hey guys, today I want to talk about…" → Good (payoff-first): "This one mistake blew up my account."
- Lead with a bold claim or curiosity gap *before* the thumb moves.

## Captions (single highest-ROI fix if missing)
- Captioned videos get ~12% more watch time and are **80% more likely to be watched to completion**.
- Adding captions can deliver up to a **95% engagement lift on TikTok**.
- Best style for education: **bold white text with one highlighted word** (Hormozi style), word-by-word.
- 80%+ of viewers watch on mute — captions are not optional.

## Engagement
- Healthy short-form engagement rate: **4–8%** (YouTube Shorts leads ~5.9%).
- **Educational content performs ~40% better than purely promotional** content.
- A clear CTA / open loop at the end lifts comments, which the algorithm reads as a quality signal.

## What top trading/finance creators do (reference set)
- **Taylor Price (@pricelesstay, ~1.1M):** Gen-Z framing, plain language, identity hooks.
- **S. Joseph Burns / New Trader U (~776K):** psychology + discipline over hype; builds trust → converts.
- **Common thread:** real charts for credibility, AI/plain script for clarity, animated captions always, daily cadence, TikTok as the gateway into a longer ecosystem (YouTube, community, signals).

## Credibility rule for trading content
Use **real chart footage** (e.g. TradingView screen-recording) for anything market-specific. AI-generated or fake charts destroy trust with a trading audience. Automate the words and voice; keep the market visuals real.
