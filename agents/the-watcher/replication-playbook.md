# The Watcher — Replication Playbook

How to turn a reviewed video into a higher-view rebuild for **Sri Thesigan** (@tan_srithesigan). The Watcher uses this to produce the "rebuild" section of every review.

## The rebuild always contains
1. **3 rewritten hooks** (<12 words each), one per style:
   - **Identity:** "If you're a forex trader under 30, stop doing this."
   - **Contrarian:** "Everyone closes too early. I never sell my last layer."
   - **Open loop:** "This one candle told me to hold. Here's why."
   Pick the strongest for *this* topic and say why.
2. **First-3-seconds on-screen text** — the words that appear in frame 0:00.
3. **Tightened script** cut to **21–34s**, one idea only, written as voiceover lines.
4. **Caption + hashtags** (see mixes below).
5. **Visual plan** — real chart footage vs. talking head, caption style, B-roll.
6. **CTA / open loop** to earn comments.
7. **A named, repeatable format** so Sri can mass-produce it.

## Proven formats for trading education (name one per rebuild)
- **Myth → Reveal → Proof:** "Most traders think X" → "Actually Y" → show the chart. Great for contrarian hooks.
- **Mistake → Cost → Fix:** name the error, show what it costs, give the one fix. High save-rate.
- **Before/After Setup:** intraday setup that became a swing — literally Sri's "layering" content, but hook-first.
- **One Number:** anchor the whole video on a single figure ("$500 vs $10k deposit").
- **Watch-me-think:** narrate a live chart read for 25s. Builds trust (the New Trader U model).

## Hook rewrite bank (adapt, don't reuse verbatim)
- "Stop closing your trades early — do this instead."
- "The layer I never sell (and why it prints)."
- "Depositing $10k? Watch this before you do."
- "Your intraday setup is secretly a swing trade."
- "90% of traders blow accounts on this one habit."

## Caption + hashtag mixes (Malaysia + forex audience)
Keep captions curiosity-driven, not descriptive. Mix tag tiers: **niche + geo + broad.**
- Niche: `#forextrader #forexmalaysia #tansrifintech #pricetaction #swingtrading`
- Geo: `#foryoumalaysia #malaysiatiktok #tradermalaysia`
- Broad: `#forex #trading #millionaires #fyp`
Rotate 5–8 per post; don't paste all 12.

## Production defaults (his faceless/hybrid pipeline)
- **Visual:** real TradingView screen-recording for the market point + optional talking-head hook.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs or CapCut TTS if faceless.
- **Captions:** CapCut auto-captions, bold white + one highlighted keyword, word-by-word.
- **Length:** 21–34s. Cut ruthlessly.
- **Cadence:** 1/day beats 3/week; one format, many topics.
- **Cross-post:** same vertical export → TikTok, Reels, Shorts, FB. (Buffer to schedule.)

## The one-line north star
Real chart for credibility, hook-first for reach, captions for the mute majority, one idea per clip, daily. Everything else is optimization.
