# Template: Origin-Story Arc

Use when: a discipline/psychology lesson needs credibility — the story licenses the lesson.
Use sparingly (the same origin retold too often becomes a brand jingle).
Emotion: earned hope or pride/identity. CTA: identity-based follow or engagement question.

```
[Hook — the low point or unglamorous starting condition. Specific, not dramatic.]

[The struggle — 2-4 sentences. What was actually hard, including the boring parts: the losing
streak, the small account, the job that funded the account. Admit what you didn't know.]

[The turn — the moment/decision that changed the trajectory. In credible stories this is a
RULE or HABIT, not a magic setup. Name it.]

[Where it led — the honest outcome. Understate it; the research's cautionary tales (FTC
action, fraud conviction) were both overclaimed-outcome stories.]

[The lesson, generalized to the reader — one or two lines.]

[CTA — identity + continuity follow, or a question about the reader's own starting point.]
```

Notes:
- Unglamorous is the register: "two years of losing" builds more trust than "then I found the
  secret." The turn must be discipline/process — never a product, indicator, or signal group.
- No income claims, no "from RM0 to RMX" framing (that's the milestone template, which carries
  its own heavy cautions).
- The persona's own origin story must be real (operator-supplied) or the post switches to the
  third-party template with attribution.
