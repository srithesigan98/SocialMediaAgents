# Template: Market-Condition Read

Use when: commenting on current volatility/sentiment/rotation. Always resolves into behavior
(sizing, patience, selectivity) — never a prediction flex.
Emotion: loss aversion (usually) or belonging. CTA: engagement question.

```
[Hook — the current condition, stated as its consequence for traders, not as news.]

[The read — 2-4 sentences: what the tape is doing, with [PLACEHOLDER] slots for live numbers
the operator fills before posting. Name the trap the condition sets: boredom-sizing in quiet
tapes, FOMO in fast ones, revenge entries after gaps.]

[The behavior — what Blue Hulk is doing differently this week because of it. Concrete: "same
size, same stops, fewer trades." Never "I think we go up/down."]

[CTA — ask for the reader's rule for this condition.]
```

Example shape:
```
The quietest weeks do the most damage.

[INDEX] has moved less than [X]% in [N] days. Everyone's bored — and boredom in this business
has a price tag: sizes creep up, stops get wider "because nothing's moving anyway."

Low volatility doesn't mean low risk. It means risk is on sale and somebody's stocking up
without noticing.

This week: same size, same stops, fewer trades. When the range breaks — it always breaks —
I'd rather be under-positioned and awake than leveraged and surprised.

What's your rule for slow markets?
```

Notes:
- **Never invent live market numbers.** Every current statistic is a [PLACEHOLDER] the operator
  fills with real data before posting — this is the template where fabrication risk is highest.
- Timely posts decay fast: post same-day or reslot.
- MY variant pairs naturally (US events at Malaysian night hours — see `malaysian_post.md`).
