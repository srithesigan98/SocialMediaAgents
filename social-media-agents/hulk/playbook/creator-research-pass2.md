**Method:** Same as pass 1 (see `creator-research.md`) — direct threads.net/threads.com fetches
return HTTP 403 for unauthenticated requests, so findings come from WebSearch-indexed post

# Synthesis

## New patterns not covered by the pass-1 playbook

1. **Anti-scam / anti-impersonation trust-signal.** Bio-level or post-level disclaimer against
   impersonation — @cryptobacker ("I will never DM you first...send money/crypto"),
   @cryptowendyo ("I DIDN'T DM YOU"). Appeared twice, independently, only among crypto creators —
   crypto-specific, not seen on stock-market accounts in this batch.
2. **Ritual/cadence "appointment content" hook.** @hyperstocks's weekly-watchlist opener ("Our
   favorite time of the week") — trades on recurrence/habit, distinct from a one-off hook.
3. **Historical/generational compounding reveal.** @13finance's Wipro post — a subtype of
   progress-reveal built on real historical/public data rather than the poster's own trading.
4. **Direct audience-question / poll hook.** @investments' "what hype stocks are you avoiding?"
   — offers no information, asks the audience to supply content. Distinct from curiosity-gap
   (which withholds information).
5. **False-binary-rejection hook.** @bitcoin.daily — stage two opposing camps, reject both,
   present a third position. A named subtype of contrarian reframe.
6. **Short-declarative-sentence closing rhythm.** Not a hook but a tone/voice device — punchy,
   quotable closing lines built for screenshot-sharing (@bitcoin.daily).

## Existing patterns reinforced

- **Numbers-first hook** — strongly reinforced (@investments, @13finance, @kobeissiletter,
  @hyperstocks).
- **Conditional/contrarian reframe** — reinforced (@investments, @bitcoin.daily).
- **Standalone aphorism** — reinforced (@bitcoin.daily's closers, @13finance's title line).
- **Progress-reveal framework** — reinforced and extended (@13finance's historical version and
  personal-portfolio version).
- **Call → reasoning → risk caveat** — reinforced (@hyperstocks, @kobeissiletter).
- **Listicle/bulleted breakdown** — reinforced (@humphreytalks, @your.richbff).
- **Position/"bags" disclosure** — not directly re-confirmed, but clusters with the new
  anti-scam-disclaimer pattern as a trust-building device for crypto accounts specifically.
- **Curiosity-gap + emoji** and **confession-then-lesson** — not strongly re-confirmed this pass;
  worth another look in a future research pass.

## Stock-market vs. crypto creators — differences observed

- **Trust/scam signaling** is crypto-specific in this batch — no stock-market account showed it.
- **Cycle/correlation framing** (referencing prior halving/bear-market cycles) is a distinctly
  crypto-native analytical device (@bitcoin.daily) with no clean stock-market equivalent here.
- **Regional specialization** showed up on the stock-market side (@13finance is India-focused —
  rupee amounts, NSE-listed stocks); the crypto accounts found were more globally/US-generic.
- **Ritual/cadence content** (weekly watchlist) appeared on the stock-market side only in this
  batch — may just be a search-coverage gap rather than a true absence on the crypto side.
- **Platform-investment disparity** (not a stock-vs-crypto split): several creators with huge X
  or TikTok followings have surprisingly small Threads followings (@kobeissiletter: 2.7K on
  Threads vs. 1.2M on X; @investments: 35.9K vs. 680K TikTok) — Threads is a secondary/repurposing
  channel for a meaningful share of finance creators, which is useful context for what "viral on
  Threads" realistically looks like, and suggests room for a Threads-native strategy to
  outperform accounts that are just cross-posting.
- **Broader "financial literacy" accounts** (@humphreytalks, @your.richbff, @kylascan) — covering
  investing/economics without specific trade calls — had the *largest* confirmed Threads
  followings in the batch (126K and 52.4K), outperforming the narrower trade-call accounts. Broad
  financial-literacy framing may travel better on Threads specifically than narrow trade-call
  content.
