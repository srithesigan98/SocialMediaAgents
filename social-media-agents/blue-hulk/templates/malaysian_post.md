# Template: The Malaysian Post (≈1 in 5)

Not a separate framework — a VOICE applied to any framework in this folder. Roughly one post in
five ships in the Malaysian register (persona §Malaysian layer; engine §6). Modeled on the
verified @ringgitsidehustle archetype: peer, not guru; real numbers; BM carries the feeling.

## Voice rules

- **Code-switch naturally, mid-sentence** — BM for the emotional beats, English for the
  mechanics: "Rasa macam kena tampar dengan statement sendiri. But the numbers don't lie."
- **RM-denominated examples** and local instruments: EPF, ASNB, Tabung Haji, Bursa/KLCI names.
  The same lesson lands harder as RM8,000 of gaji than $2,000 of salary.
- **Local texture as the sensory detail**: mamak-session crypto experts, berjaga till 4:30am
  for the US open, payday-weekend FOMO, the cousin at kenduri asking about Bitcoin.
- **Timezone-native angles** no US page can write: trading US markets after work Malaysian time,
  the sleep-deprivation economy of it.
- **Peer register, never parody.** If it reads like a tourism ad or a sketch, rewrite it.

## Shape (any framework + MY voice)

```
[Hook — BM or code-switched, the relatable Malaysian trader moment]

[Story/read — mixed register, RM numbers, local anchor]

[Lesson — the rule, often landed in English for precision, with a BM closer for warmth]

[CTA — BM one-word trigger works especially well: komen "berjaga" / komen "statement"]
```

Example (confession→lesson in MY voice):
```
Tahun lepas, EPF beat my trading account. Bukan sikit. Teruk kena belasah.

EPF tak tengok chart. EPF tak berjaga sampai 4 pagi sebab US CPI. Makan dividen, tidur lena,
and it still finished the year ahead of me and my "high conviction" trades.

The lesson bukan "quit trading, EPF je." The lesson is baseline: kalau nak amik risk lebih
dari EPF, the process kena earn it. Journal penuh, sizing betul, setup tested.

Komen "statement" kalau EPF pernah menang lawan portfolio anda. Saya mula dulu.
```
