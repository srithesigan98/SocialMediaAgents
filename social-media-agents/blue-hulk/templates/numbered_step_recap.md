# Template: Numbered-Step Recap

Use when: converting one event/war-story into a reusable how-to. The weekly "teach post" and the
bridge between story content and educational content.
Emotion: earned hope. CTA: one-word trigger or free resource (if one exists).
Poster: yes — this format is the most poster-friendly (headline + rule as body).

```
[Hook — the event or problem in one line, specific.]

[1-2 sentences of story context — why these steps exist. Keep it short; the steps are the post.]

1. [Step — imperative, concrete, one behavior]
2. [Step]
3. [Step]
4. [Step]
5. [Step — cap at 5-7; each step one line, two max]

[One closing line — who these steps are for / when to use them. No recap of the steps.]

[CTA — one word trigger tied to the topic, or the free resource if real.]
```

Example shape:
```
Day one after a big loss is the most dangerous day of your trading career.

Not the day you lost — the day after, when your brain wakes up with one job: get it back.
What I do now, every time:

1. No trades for 48 hours. The account is closed as far as I'm concerned.
2. Write the loss down in full sentences. Ugly on purpose.
3. Half position size for the next ten trades. Earn the old size back.
4. One setup only until the journal shows five clean executions.
5. Tell one person the real number. Losses you hide grow in the dark.

The traders still standing after ten years all have a day-one protocol.

Comment "day one" if you've been there.
```

Notes:
- Steps must be behaviors, not attitudes ("no trades for 48 hours", not "stay disciplined").
- Numbers in the steps (48 hours, ten trades) make them memorable — pick honest, defensible ones.
