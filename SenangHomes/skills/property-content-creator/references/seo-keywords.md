# SEO Keywords & Hashtag Strategy — KL Property

## Why hashtag tiering matters

If you stack only mega-hashtags (#property, #realestate, #kualalumpur), your video drops into a feed competing with millions of posts and you drown. If you use only tiny niche tags, you reach 50 people. The play is **a deliberate mix across 3 tiers**, plus **Bahasa/local tags**, so the algorithm can find your real KL buyer audience.

Use **15–25 hashtags per post** — distributed across the tiers below. On Instagram Reels, hashtags still meaningfully aid discovery for local property, so don't skimp.

## Tier 1: Mega tags (use 2–4)

Huge but signal the broad category. Don't skip them entirely; the algorithm uses them as topic anchors.

```
#property  #realestate  #kualalumpur  #malaysia  #propertymalaysia
#homebuying  #investment  #condo  #klang valley  #hartanah
```

## Tier 2: Niche-strong tags (use 6–10) — the workhorses

Enough volume to push reach, small enough that a good video can rank inside them. **Most of your hashtag weight should be here.**

```
#propertymalaysia  #hartanahmalaysia  #condomalaysia  #kualalumpurproperty
#klproperty  #klangvalleyproperty  #firsttimehomebuyer  #firsthome
#rumahpertama  #rumahmampumilik  #propertyinvestment  #propertyinvestor
#realestatemalaysia  #condoforsale  #serviceresidence  #newlaunch
#subsale  #homeloan  #propertyagentmalaysia  #hartanahdijual
```

## Tier 3: Long-tail / community tags (use 4–8)

Small but high-intent. Shows up to people who really care — also more likely to comment, save, and enquire.

```
#firsttimebuyermalaysia  #rumahpertamaku  #belirumah  #belirumahpertama
#kondominium  #apartmentmalaysia  #propertytips  #propertytipsmalaysia
#housingloan  #loanrumah  #stampduty  #dutisetem  #spa
#mrtmalaysia  #transitorienteddevelopment  #tod
#cheras  #montkiara  #bangsar  #oldklangroad  #setapak  #sentul
#bukitjalil  #kepong  #wangsamaju  #sripetaling  #sungaibesi
```

Swap the area tags to match the actual area in the video — local area tags are some of the highest-intent reach you can get.

## Bahasa / local language tags (use 3–5) — don't skip these

A big slice of the KL first-home audience scrolls and searches in Bahasa Malaysia and Manglish. These tags reach buyers the pure-English tags miss:

```
#hartanah  #hartanahmalaysia  #hartanahdijual  #rumahpertama
#rumahmampumilik  #belirumah  #rumahdijual  #rumahku
#kondomalaysia  #hartanahkl  #panduanbelirumah
```

## Caption SEO — the often-missed part

The caption itself (not just hashtags) influences what the platform decides the video is "about." Front-load 1–2 keyword phrases naturally, and include the **price and area** — those are what people search:

**Bad caption:** "Watch till the end 😍🏠"

**Good caption:** "RM450k condo in Old Klang Road, 6 minutes to the MRT — here's the honest breakdown for first-time buyers in KL. Comment your budget 👇"

Notice the good one packs in: "RM450k condo", "Old Klang Road", "MRT", "first-time buyers in KL" — four searchable phrases, all natural, plus a comment prompt. Mixing in a Bahasa phrase ("worth it tak?") can lift local reach further.

## Sound / audio SEO

Trending audio is a discovery signal:
- Use **trending Malaysian sounds** for hooks/transitions (mute under your voiceover).
- Or use **trending sounds inside the local property/finance/lifestyle FYP** to land on the right FYP.
- Check Instagram/TikTok's trending audio weekly; localised trending sounds beat global generic ones for a KL-targeted account.

## What NOT to do

- Don't repeat the same 25 hashtags on every post — looks spammy and can get de-ranked as low-effort. Rotate, especially the area tags.
- Don't use banned/"shadowed" hashtags — search the tag; if no recent posts show, avoid it.
- Don't stuff hashtags into the spoken script. They go in the caption only.
- Don't rely on #fyp / #foryou / #viral — they signal low-effort content and don't help.
- Don't forget the area tag — for a hyper-local brand like Senang Homes, the neighbourhood tag is often the single most valuable one on the post.
