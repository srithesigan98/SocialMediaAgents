# Senang Homes — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Senang Homes (Instagram and Threads: **@senanghomes**) uses a small automation app, "Hulk", to
publish its own property posts and to answer comments left on those posts. This page explains
what that app does with your information.

## What we access

When you comment on a Senang Homes post on Instagram or Threads, the app reads:

- the comment's ID, text and time, and
- your public username.

We only read comments on our own posts. We do not access your profile, followers, messages
(other than the reply we send you) or any other account data.

## How we use it

Only to answer your comment: a public reply under it and, on Instagram, a private reply (DM).
The reply carries details about the property and a WhatsApp link so you can contact our agent.

## What we keep

- **Comment IDs**, so we never answer the same comment twice. No names or comment text are
  stored with them.
- **Operational logs** of each run (which may include your username and the comment text) are
  kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## Data deletion

To have your information removed, send a direct message to **@senanghomes** on Instagram with
your username. We will delete any records linked to your comments within 30 days and confirm
by message. You can also delete your comment on Instagram or Threads at any time.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: message **@senanghomes** on Instagram.
