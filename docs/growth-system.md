# Audience Research — forex / gold trader (filled)

Grounded in the @tan_srithesigan audience: retail forex/gold traders and aspiring
traders, Malaysia-first. Use these to write hooks, captions, and offers.

### 🗂️ Demographics
- Age **18–40**, skew **22–32**. Majority **male (~75%)**, growing female segment.
- **Malaysia core** (KL, Johor, Penang), plus SG, Indonesia, India, MENA diaspora.
- Mobile-first, English + Malay, employed or side-hustling; modest starting capital ($100–$2k).

### 🗂️ Psychographics
- Wants **financial freedom** and to escape the 9–5; admires the "trader lifestyle."
- Self-taught via TikTok/YouTube; distrusts banks, attracted to fast results.
- Identity-driven: sees trading as a path to status and independence, not just income.

### 🗂️ Pain Points
- **Blowing accounts** from over-leverage and revenge trading.
- No consistent **strategy or risk management**; emotional decisions.
- Overwhelmed by conflicting "gurus"; can't tell real edge from hype.
- Small capital → feels progress is too slow to matter.

### 🗂️ Desires & Aspirations
- **Consistent, repeatable** profits; a system they can trust.
- Prove doubters wrong; quit the day job; provide for family.
- Belong to a credible trader community, not a scam.

### 🗂️ Frequently Asked Questions
- "How much capital do I need to start?"
- "Is gold (XAU) better than forex pairs for beginners?"
- "What's the safest lot size / risk per trade?"
- "How do I stop over-trading and revenge trading?"
- "Which broker / prop firm should I use?"

### 🗂️ Objections
- "This is probably another scam / guru selling a course."
- "I don't have enough money to trade."
- "I've tried before and lost — why is this different?"
- "I don't have time to sit at charts all day."

### 🗂️ Buying Triggers
- **Proof**: real chart footage of the setup working (credibility > claims).
- A **specific, teachable technique** (e.g. layering, risk-per-trade rule).
- Free value first → small paid step (signals/community) → mentorship.
- Social proof: student wins, testimonials, a visible community.

### 🗂️ Audience Insights
- **Real charts beat talking heads** — trading audiences trust screen-recorded proof.
- **Education outperforms hype** by ~40%; discipline/psychology content builds trust that converts.
- **Hook in <3s, captions always** (80%+ watch muted); one lesson per video.
- Malaysia geo-tags (#foreignMalaysia style) widen local reach; mix niche + geo + broad hashtags.
- Daily cadence + one repeatable format beats sporadic posting.
